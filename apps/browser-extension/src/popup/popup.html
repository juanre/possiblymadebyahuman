Writing records

Help

Click in the field you want to work on, then choose Start below. You can also right-click it and choose “Start writing record”, or press Alt+Shift+W. The field can already contain text.

A writing record contains editing measurements, not your words. Stop to pause a draft; resume it later in the chosen field. Finishing publishes a record and stops capture.

Records edits from when you start. Earlier writing is not included.
